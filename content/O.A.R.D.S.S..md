The O.A.R.D.S.S. or Orbital Autonomous Records and Data Storage System is a man made celestial body its purpose is to store all knowledge added to the [[I.S.D.]] in other words, it's a massive server in space. It contains all of the information in the [[I.S.D.]] as well as its own [[Fusion reactor]] so far there is only one in existence as it requires making extensive modifications to an [[Orbital forge]]  takes thousands of tons of rare materials as well as years to assemble even with [[Nexus Engineering Technologies|N.E.T.]]'s highly advanced [[Nanotech]].
