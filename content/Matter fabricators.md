Matter fabricators are the primary means of manufacturing used by [[Nexus Engineering Technologies|N.E.T.]] they come in different sises suitable for different applications ranging from household to industrial to orbital [[Orbital forge|orbital forges]]. They work by using [[Nanobots|nanobots]] to construct objects at the atomic level they are capable of fabricating anything the user can design or find a design for, the first iteration was quite similar to FDM 3D printers of the early 21st century, after the advent of [[Nanotech|nanotechnology]] they improved in accuracy reliability and speed and are now built into the homes of ever [[N.E.T. employee]].
