The I.N.A.P. (internal neural access  port) is a neural implant that is installed in all employees of N.E.T. It is used to access the interplanetary network, any employee may use it to play games, write notes, develop apps, design machines, tools etc. In other words its your phone computer and every other personal device you could need all within a chip installed in your head!
