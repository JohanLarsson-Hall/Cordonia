# **Capitalism**

### **Means of production**
Privately owned all additional profits go to the owner

### **Profit Motive**
Be better than the competition to make more money

### **Labor**
Everyone works at the factories often for meager pay including  men women and children

### **Key figures**
* Adam Smith a Scottish economist and philosopher who advocated for free trade
* Andrew Carnegie a Scottish-American industrialist  and steel magnate  who believed that money should go to rich people that are smarter as they have more insight and can use it better
* William Graham Sumner believed economic inequality is a natural law and that survival of the fittest applies to the economy
### **Summarize what you’ve learned on this page**
Capitalism focused on profit at all cost and is primarily advantageous to those who own the means of production 

# **Socialism**

### **Means of Production**
 Owned by the community, workers control production democratically

### **Labor**
Worker labor create value so workers should own the machines and make the decision

### **Main critique of Capitalism**
Owners do nothing but get paid the most

### **Key figures**
* Karl Marx a German philosopher, economist, and revolutionary. Wrote the communist manifesto and the capital, argues that history is a struggle of classes.
* Friedrich Engels a German philosopher and Marx’s collaborator
* Eugene V. Debs american labour leader and socialist organizer

### **Key similarities and differences between Capitalism and Socialism**
Capitalism is about owning and producing things and using those things to make profit 
While Socialism is about letting those who work own the machines and distributing profits based on need
