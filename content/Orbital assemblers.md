### Summary
---
Orbital assemblers are orbital platforms that use a combination of [[Drones|drones]] and [[Nanobots|nanobots]] to build large objects such as [[Ships|ships]] they can also be used to build [[Space stations|space stations]] though they must be orbiting the same planet that the space station will be orbiting.

---
### functionality

Orbital assemblers are different than [[Orbital forge|orbital forges]] they do not manufacture parts instead they take the parts produced the by [[Orbital forge|orbital forges]] and put them together this does not mean that an [[Orbital forge|orbital forge]] is incapable of fabricating a fully functional object however some things are large then the build volume of an [[Orbital forge|orbital forge]] or are simply not practical to fabricate using a singular forge.
