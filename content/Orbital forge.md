Orbital forges are a class of [[Matter fabricators]]. they are too large to be used on a planet and are therefore used in space, hence the name orbital forge they are used for making parts for [[Ships|ships]], [[Space stations|space stations]], [[Jump gates|jump gates]] and [[Orbital assemblers|orbital assemblers]]. They may also be retrofitted to construct an [[O.A.R.D.S.S.]] however due to the complexity of the [[O.A.R.D.S.S.]] the retrofit is designed to self uninstall after completion.
